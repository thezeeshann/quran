القرآن الكريم

The Quran, beautifully.

A fast, native Quran reader for the Mac. The Uthmani text beautifully typeset, read by surah or by juz, with translations, instant search, and highlights, notes and bookmarks kept on your computer.

Download for Mac
Version 0.1.0 · Apple Silicon and Intel · Free · Source on GitHub

Typeset for reading

The Uthmani text in Amiri Quran, set right to left word by word, with every ayah closed by its ornament. Light, sepia and dark themes.

Surah and juz

Browse all 114 surahs or the 30 juz by name. Read a juz at a time, with its details at the top and the next one a click away.

Translations

Saheeh International, Pickthall, Yusuf Ali, transliteration and Urdu built in. Over 100 more in about 45 languages, one click each.

Instant search

Search the translation or the Arabic as you type. Harakat and letter forms are folded, so الصبر finds every form.

Keyboard first

⌘K jumps anywhere, ← → turn surahs, ⇧← ⇧→ turn juz, ⌘F searches, 1–6 highlight.

Yours, and native

Highlights, notes, bookmarks and reading plans stay in a local file on your Mac. Written in Rust: small, quiet and smooth.

Installing

  1. Open Quran.dmg and drag Quran into Applications.
  2. Open Quran. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the developer: click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Quran, and confirm. You only do this once.

Or skip the dialog: after dragging Quran into Applications, run this in Terminal.
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Quran.app
